How it works

The steel is modelled member by member from the structural and architectural drawings, then checked twice before it reaches you.

1

Send the drawings

Structural and architectural PDFs. You receive a fixed price and a delivery time the same day.

2

Modelled and checked

The frame, secondary steel and connections are modelled in Tekla Structures, then put through an independent review against the drawings and automatic checks for double counts and gaps.

3

Reviewed and issued

An estimator with Australian steel estimating and detailing experience reviews the result and signs it off. Revisions for drawing updates are tracked issue by issue.

About

Who checks your take-off.

Estimating, drafting and project coordination

Estimator and Project Coordinator with a structural steel drafting background and over 9 years of experience across structural steel fabrication, mechanical, construction and mining projects. Master of Mechanical Engineering graduate and Engineers Australia member.

Experience includes interpreting tender drawings and specifications, preparing material take-offs (MTOs) and cost estimates, compiling RFQs and evaluating subcontractor and supplier quotations, procuring and scheduling materials, quality documentation, and coordinating projects from tender through fabrication to delivery.

Modelling and drafting

Every take-off is built as a 3D model in Tekla Structures from the structural and architectural drawings, to Australian sections and the engineer's standard connection details. The same model produces the bill of materials, the IFC file and the query list, so the quantities can be checked member by member against the drawings.

Detailed tender model

An option for jobs where the model is shown to the client or carried into detailing: main members are cut to the faces they land on, and the main connections are modelled from the engineer's standard details instead of a weight allowance.

Eave: end plate, column stiffeners and diagonal stiffener at the thicknesses on the connection details.
Truss: chord splices and webs cut to the chord faces, chord ends plated to the column.
